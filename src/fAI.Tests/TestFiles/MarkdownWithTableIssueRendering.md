## 2. Why the "Degenerative" Diagnosis Is Insufficient
Lumbar spondylosis, facet arthropathy and an annular tear are near-universal MRI findings in adults and are **not** an adequate explanation for:
- Pain that abolishes ambulation for months
- Only 26 hours of relief from a corticosteroid injection
- A **new** bony tender point appearing over one week
- A frank **L4 fracture** in a patient with no reported trauma
- Clinical concern high enough to warrant a **bone biopsy**
In a dialysis patient, a vertebral fracture without significant trauma is by definition a **pathologic / insufficiency  fracture** and must be attributed to an underlying bone-quality or destructive process.
